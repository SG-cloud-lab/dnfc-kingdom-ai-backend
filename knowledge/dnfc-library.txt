The Eternal Life

God-Man Union



The Lord Jesus Christ came into this earth to demonstrate and give the LIFE, this life is beyond the ordinary human life because it is God’s life. 

The Lord lived on earth as both God and man, meaning He was on earth hundred percent God and hundred percent human. 

The Bible says, 1Tim 3:16: "And without controversy great is the mystery of godliness: God was manifest in the flesh…" The mystery of godliness lies in the revelation or revealing of God in the flesh. This verse speaks of the Lord Jesus when He came in the flesh that was the manifestation of God in the flesh. 

The Bible says in the book of John 1:1: “In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.” This last sentence says, “and the Word was God.” 

Now when you go to verse 14 of the same chapter, it says and the Word was made flesh and dwelt among us. This is what Apostle Paul named as the mystery of godliness. It’s the God-man union. God coming in the flesh. 

When He came on earth as both God and man, He had come to unite man with God by giving man the God-Life. 

Looking at the life of the Lord Jesus on earth, He commanded sicknesses out of people’s bodies, He casted out demons, He commanded the winds and storms. He raised the dead, commanded the nature. 

At one point after speaking to the winds, the Bible says, He disciples asked themselves saying, who is this man who can even speak to the wind and it obeys His order.

 Listen, Jesus had the DNA of God in Himself and walked in the earth as was defined in His spirit. He lived in the world beyond the ordinary because He had in Himself the life of God. At one point He said, John 5:26: “For as the Father has life in himself; so has he given to the Son to have life in himself;” 

I want you to pay attention here, God does not have life in Himself but God is life Himself, this is exactly what this verse is saying. The second part that says, “so has He given to the Son to have life in Himself” means, God is the very life in the Son.

Right from the beginning God wanted to be the life in man, this is the only way by which a man can become an expression of God in this earth. The Bible speaking of the Lord Jesus says, He is the visible image of the invisible God. Col 1::15.

Jesus on earth was a God-man, He carried the DNA of God in Himself, no wonder they said, what manner of a man is this, they noticed that He was a different kind of man, little did they know that He was a God-man. He told them that He that has seen me has seen my Father. That’s the God-man. 

The appearing of the Lord Jesus Christ on earth was a divine invitation to bring man to this God-Life. The Bible said, John 10:10:… I am come that they might have life..." He came to bring man in God’s class, God’s realm. The preaching of the gospel is the divine invitation from God towards humanity into the God-life, into the God experiences. 

Through this God-life you are recreated in Christ to bear the image and the likeness of God. In Christ we are made new species, God’s breed and God-kind of man. We are the God-man in Christ called to live a life that when people looks at us they’ll say, what manner of people are these. 

This life that the Lord came to give brings man into deeper union with the Godhead, man becomes a replica of God, an expression and revelation of the Godhead on earth. 

Many of God’s children doesn’t give themselves to understanding the kind of life the Lord Jesus came to give, that’s why they live natural lives. The Bible says, Prov 4:7: “…with all your getting get understanding.”

This means, over everything that given to you or you get, you must carry a certain depth of understanding of that thing. In this book, I want us to explore and understand the God-Life that makes us God-man. The coming together of God and man.

Eternal Life



Eternal life is God’s boundless gift towards humanity. Not only is eternal life God’s life in human vessels but the very eternal life is God being the life of man. Eternal life is God gifting Himself to man as their life. 

The Lord in one of the scriptures said, John 14:6: “Jesus said to him, I am the way, the truth, AND THE LIFE: no man comes to the Father, but by me.” He is the very life in us. So eternal life is God gifting humans as their life. 

Many time we say eternal life is God’s life which is true, but while we look at the revelation of Christ, we can understand that not only is eternal life God’s life in us but it is God being man’s life. The Bible says in the book of Col 3:4: “When Christ, who is our life…” Christ is our life. In the book of John 11:25,He said, I am the resurrection and the LIFE. Another scripture says,  John 10:10 …I have come that they may have life, and have it to the full. Having life to the full is the fullness of God residing and finding expression through a human vessel.

Thus when we touch God in Christ as the Spirit, we touch life, for life is God in Christ as the Spirit. The Father is the source of life. The Son is the course of life. The Spirit is the flow of life. 

Life is the Father in the Son as the Spirit flowing into us to be our life and life supply. This is how we are joined to Him, we share the same life. 

Life is this processed Triune God flowing into us with all His divine riches, all that He has accomplished, passed through, obtained, and attained, to deal with all the negative things in us and to fill us up with Himself.

That which filled Christ, which is the Godhead fills us now and is now being expressed through us. This life is the light of men. The Bible says, John 1:4: "In him(Jesus Christ) was life; and the life was the light of men." 

That same life which is God is also men’s light, meaning that when a men receives this life, he must learn to live by the revelation and wisdom of that life. God is the revelation of man, and until men contact and see God, they continue in ignorance and confusion because God is the life-light of men. 

So eternal life is the Godhead filling a men, it is God becoming men’s life and light. Eternal life is the highest revelation of God towards men. In relation to Himself, God does not need to say that He is life.
