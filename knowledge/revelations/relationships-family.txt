DNFC REVELATIONS: RELATIONSHIPS AND FAMILY


This section contains teachings about relationships, marriage, family, and godly connections.


TOPICS:

- Relationships
- Marriage


PURPOSE:

To provide biblical understanding of covenant relationships, love, honour, responsibility, and God's design for human connections.


SCRIPTURE FOUNDATION:

Genesis 2:24
Ephesians 5:25
Colossians 3:14

TITLE: Marriage, Covenant, and Divine Purpose


CORE TEACHING:

God does not join a man and woman in marriage without divine purpose.

Marriage is more than companionship; it is a covenant partnership designed to fulfill God's plan.


KINGDOM UNDERSTANDING:

When purpose is understood, marriage is viewed as a God-ordained union connected to destiny.

A marriage built on divine purpose carries greater commitment, responsibility, and understanding.


SPIRITUAL APPLICATION:

Approach marriage as a covenant before God and seek His purpose within the relationship.

A kingdom marriage exists to glorify God and advance His purposes.


SELAH:
God's design for relationships is greater than human understanding.
