DNFC REVELATIONS: KINGDOM SUCCESS AND WISDOM


This section contains teachings about kingdom principles, prosperity, stewardship, wisdom, and spiritual principles of increase.


TOPICS:

- Success
- Sowing and Reaping
- Wisdom


PURPOSE:

To teach believers God's principles for fruitful living, wise decisions, diligence, stewardship, and kingdom advancement.


SCRIPTURE FOUNDATION:

Proverbs 10:4
Galatians 6:7
James 1:5

TITLE: Fruitfulness, Increase, and Faithful Stewardship


CORE TEACHING:

God called believers to bear fruit that remains.

Increase and multiplication are connected to maintaining what God has entrusted to you and faithfully adding to it.


KINGDOM UNDERSTANDING:

Obedience and faithfulness in small things carry great weight in defining the character of your anointing.

Many people overlook small acts of obedience, yet God uses them to establish greater responsibilities.


SPIRITUAL APPLICATION:

Hold firmly to every truth received from God.

Cultivate discipline, stewardship, and faithfulness in every area of life.


SELAH:
Faithfulness prepares you for greater kingdom responsibility.

TITLE: Fruit That Remains


CORE TEACHING:

God called us to bear fruit that remains.

When Jesus spoke about fruit that remains, He revealed God's desire that what He births in us and through us should continue, grow, and endure.

Heaven does not only celebrate the fruit we produce; it also celebrates the fruit we preserve.

A full reward is not only found in achieving or receiving something from God, but also in faithfully maintaining what has been entrusted to us.


KINGDOM UNDERSTANDING:

God desires that whatever we build and invest in is preserved.

Whether it is a business, ministry, relationship, opportunity, or spiritual progress, it is God's pattern that His fruit remains.

Repeatedly losing what God has entrusted to you is not His design. The Spirit of God teaches believers how to establish foundations that cannot be shaken and systems that endure.


SPIRITUAL APPLICATION:

Learn to maintain what God has given you.

Develop wisdom, discipline, and stewardship so that the blessings, opportunities, and assignments entrusted to you continue to produce fruit.


SELAH:
Grace produces fruit, but wisdom preserves it.

TITLE: The Anointing Must Produce Fruit


CORE TEACHING:

You are born again and a partaker of the divine nature. You carry the presence and anointing of God.

The presence of God upon your life should produce visible evidence that He is with you.


KINGDOM UNDERSTANDING:

The anointing of God is not merely an experience; it carries divine purpose and produces results.

When the Holy Spirit is resident in a person, His presence produces transformation that can be seen and experienced.

A life that carries God's presence should demonstrate the fruit and evidence of that presence.


SPIRITUAL APPLICATION:

Do not settle for merely carrying spiritual experiences.

Allow the Spirit of God to produce character, wisdom, effectiveness, and results through your life.


SELAH:
The presence of God announces the person who carries Him.

TITLE: True Prosperity Begins With God


CORE TEACHING:

True prosperity begins with a relationship with God.

God's measure of success goes beyond material achievements, public recognition, and the approval of people.

His purpose is not merely survival but reigning according to His divine plan.


KINGDOM UNDERSTANDING:

Seek first the Kingdom of God and His righteousness.

When the heart is correctly aligned with God, every other area of life comes into proper order.

Prosperity is not only having resources; it is having the wisdom, character, and relationship with God required to steward those resources.


SPIRITUAL APPLICATION:

Set your priorities according to God's Kingdom.

Allow your relationship with God to become the foundation of every pursuit.


SELAH:
Kingdom prosperity begins in the heart before it appears in the hands.

TITLE: Honour, Giving, and Kingdom Stewardship


CORE TEACHING:

Honour and worship should be expressed through giving.

God delights in receiving from a reverent giver who gives intentionally and with a cheerful heart.


KINGDOM UNDERSTANDING:

Giving is not merely a transaction; it is an expression of honour, faith, and relationship with God.

God sees every act of obedience performed with the right heart and rewards what is done according to His principles.


SPIRITUAL APPLICATION:

Give with understanding, honour God with your resources, and allow generosity to become part of your kingdom lifestyle.


SELAH:
True giving begins with honour before it begins with resources.
