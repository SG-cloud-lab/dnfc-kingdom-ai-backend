DNFC REVELATIONS: KINGDOM SUCCESS AND WISDOM


This section contains teachings about kingdom principles, prosperity, stewardship, wisdom, and spiritual principles of increase.


TOPICS:

- Success
- Sowing and Reaping
- Wisdom


PURPOSE:

To teach believers God's principles for fruitful living, wise decisions, diligence, stewardship, and kingdom advancement.


SCRIPTURE FOUNDATION:

Proverbs 10:4
Galatians 6:7
James 1:5

TITLE: Fruitfulness, Increase, and Faithful Stewardship


CORE TEACHING:

God called believers to bear fruit that remains.

Increase and multiplication are connected to maintaining what God has entrusted to you and faithfully adding to it.


KINGDOM UNDERSTANDING:

Obedience and faithfulness in small things carry great weight in defining the character of your anointing.

Many people overlook small acts of obedience, yet God uses them to establish greater responsibilities.


SPIRITUAL APPLICATION:

Hold firmly to every truth received from God.

Cultivate discipline, stewardship, and faithfulness in every area of life.


SELAH:
Faithfulness prepares you for greater kingdom responsibility.
