DNFC REVELATIONS: FAITH AND BELIEVING

This section contains teachings about faith, believing God, spiritual possibilities, and walking according to the reality of God's Word.

TOPICS:

Faith
Believing
Possibilities

PURPOSE:

To teach believers how faith operates, how confidence in God produces spiritual results, and how to live beyond natural limitations through trust in God.

SCRIPTURE FOUNDATION:

Hebrews 11:1
Mark 11:24
Romans 10:17

TITLE: Faith, Revelation, and Recognizing God


CORE TEACHING:

God uses opportunities and circumstances to draw people closer to Himself.

He leaves signs and reminders of His presence to awaken faith and reveal that He is working.


KINGDOM UNDERSTANDING:

Faith grows when our spiritual eyes are opened to recognize God's activity.

We must learn to see beyond natural circumstances and recognize the ways God reveals Himself.


SPIRITUAL APPLICATION:

Do not accept anything as revelation that removes the peace God gives.

Walk with confidence, knowing that God is present and actively leading you.


SELAH:
Ask God to open your eyes to see His work.

TITLE: Faith, Revelation, and the Transformation of Consciousness


CORE TEACHING:

Faith comes by hearing, and hearing by the Word of God.

God designed humanity to perceive according to what has been heard from Him, not merely according to natural circumstances.

True faith begins when the believer receives God's perspective and agrees with His Word.


KINGDOM UNDERSTANDING:

If your consciousness has not been changed and aligned with the Word of God, the Word cannot produce its full work in your life.

Transformation begins with a renewed mind and a consciousness that agrees with God's divine pattern.

As you become acquainted with the Word of God, your mind is renewed to perceive life from the heart of God.


SPIRITUAL APPLICATION:

Deliberately surround yourself with the Word of God.

Guard your heart from negative influences and allow Scripture to shape your thoughts, beliefs, and decisions.


SELAH:
A transformed consciousness produces a transformed life.

TITLE: Faith Responds to God's Word


CORE TEACHING:

God responds to faith, not emotional manipulation.

God is compassionate, but He operates according to covenant principles and the truth of His Word.

Faith is not based on trying to convince God through emotions; it is confidence in His character, promises, and faithfulness.


KINGDOM UNDERSTANDING:

Genuine faith produces works.

Faith is demonstrated through steadfastness, obedience, and confidence in the sovereignty of God even during trials.


SPIRITUAL APPLICATION:

Build your faith through the Word of God.

Stand on God's promises and refuse to allow circumstances to determine your confidence in Him.


SELAH:
Faith rests in who God is, not only in what God does.

TITLE: Living From Covenant Identity


CORE TEACHING:

As a child of God, you carry divine inheritance.

Through Christ, believers receive a new identity, spiritual authority, and access to the resources of God's Kingdom.


KINGDOM UNDERSTANDING:

A believer must understand their covenant position and refuse to live as a victim of circumstances.

Your identity in Christ determines your approach to life, challenges, and opportunities.


SPIRITUAL APPLICATION:

Stand firm in your identity as God's child.

Think, speak, and act according to the reality of what Christ has accomplished for you.


SELAH:
Your covenant identity determines your kingdom perspective.

TITLE: The Word of God as the Divine Pattern


CORE TEACHING:

The Word of God is the divine blueprint by which believers should pattern their lives.

Reject every imitation that is outside the convictions of the Spirit and the truth of Scripture.


KINGDOM UNDERSTANDING:

Because the world is filled with corrupt influences, believers must intentionally feed on God's Word and guard their hearts.

The Word strengthens, calms, and anchors believers in truth.


SPIRITUAL APPLICATION:

Make God's Word your standard for decisions, beliefs, and actions.

Allow Scripture to shape your understanding of yourself, God, and the world around you.


SELAH:
The Word is the pattern for kingdom living.
